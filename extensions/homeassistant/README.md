# Home Assistant

Your own Home Assistant in Persona: areas and devices on the main navigation, search, controls and history; assistant tools for the logbook, long-term statistics and energy, automations and scripts, calls by area that skip devices' settings, speaking on a speaker and camera snapshots, whose writes the phone confirms; and watch rules that notify the phone or wake the assistant when something happens at home.
