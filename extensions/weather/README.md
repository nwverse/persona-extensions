# Weather

Weather in Persona from Open-Meteo's public APIs, free and without an account: the current weather, the next 24 hours as a temperature line, a 7-day forecast and current air quality, with assistant tools to read each. The hourly outlook adds European seasonal pollen concentrations and forward air-quality observations. Unavailable pollen is reported explicitly, never treated as zero, and no medical or dosage advice is given. Open-Meteo and CAMS ENSEMBLE attribution is shown on the page.
